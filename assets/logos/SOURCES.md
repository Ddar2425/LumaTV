# Logo sources

YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, and Spotify: Simple Icons 13.0.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.0.0/icons (CC0; LICENSE.md). SVG fills were changed to the corresponding brand colors.

Disney+: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/blob/main/svg/disney-plus.svg, credited to Eric P Pereira on https://dashboardicons.com/icons/disney-plus. Repository license included as DASHBOARD-LICENSE (Apache 2.0). SVG artwork was not modified.

Brand names and logos identify their respective services; they belong to their respective owners. No affiliation is implied.

All SVG assets are bundled locally for offline display. Known service domains also get their matching logo when added as custom shortcuts. Other shortcuts retain the generic icon.
